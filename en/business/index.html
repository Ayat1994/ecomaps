About

Décor for offices, salons and schools

ECOMAPS makes interior décor in your corporate style: multi-layer panels in wood, HDF and MDF, backlit Instagram mirrors, branded artwork, and world and Kazakhstan maps with your company logo. We cut, engrave, print and assemble everything ourselves in Astana.

Our clients include offices and receptions, salons, car dealerships, schools, kindergartens and kids’ centres. Before you order, we show the décor on a photo of your space for free.

We work with companies by invoice and ship to the UAE, Qatar and worldwide. When you order a map for your office, we make your company logo for free.

Q & A

Frequently asked questions

What is an Instagram mirror?

A mirror styled as an Instagram post with your account, logo, QR code and backlight. It works as a photo zone in a salon, café, shop or office.

Can we see how the décor will look in our space?

Yes. Send us a photo of the room on WhatsApp and we will make a free mock-up.

Can you work from our own design?

Yes. We have over 20 ready panel templates, and we can also work from your artwork or in your corporate style.

Do you work with companies?

Yes. We issue invoices for international bank transfer and ship to your country.

How long does production take?

About 8 working days after design approval. Custom orders are scheduled individually.

Do you deliver to the UAE and Qatar?

Yes. We ship from our workshop in Kazakhstan to the UAE, Qatar and other countries. Your manager confirms the delivery cost and timing for your address before you pay. Installation is simple: instructions and all fixings are included, and we can help over a video call.
